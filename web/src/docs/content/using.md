# Using r1os

You drive r1os with the scroll wheel and the side button. Touch helps, and leads when docked.

## Everywhere

A hold is a press of 350 ms or more; a double click is two presses within 280 ms.

| Do this | It does |
|---|---|
| **Hold** and speak | Talk to an agent; let go to send |
| **Hold + turn** | Volume; a first nudge doesn't cancel your words (over Android apps: planned) |
| **Double click**, turn up past the top and click, or tap a page's header twice | Back; from an Android app, back to r1os |
| **Swipe down from the top** | The glance screen (not over Android apps) |
| **Triple press** | Your shortcut (Settings › Controls) |

## The glance screen

Waking shows the time, status, next alarm and timer, and your last agent peeking up. In between, one page at a time: **what's waiting** (notifications, reminders, requests), **an agent at work**, unread replies, what's playing and widgets.

**Click** to sleep, **turn down** for the next page, **turn up** or double click for home, and **hold** to talk to the agent peeking up, or touch what you want.

By touch it's a sheet. Swipe up from its bottom edge and it rides up with your finger, home showing under it: let go far enough up, or flick, and you're home; else it drops back. Swipe down from the top of any r1os screen and it slides down over it, and a swipe up puts it back.

With a PIN set, it's also the lock screen: a four-reel wheel lock. A swipe up then only moves it a little and opens the lock. A double click while asleep wakes straight to home (or the lock).

## Home

Turn through your agents, then **New agent**, then your pinned apps. **Click** for your current chat with the agent on screen, **hold** to talk to it, and **double click** for its space: its chats, memory, look, brain and [what it can change](/docs/agents#approvals). A count means unread replies; the agent's dot pulses while it works.

Turn up past the first agent to sleep. Turn down past the end, or swipe up from the bottom edge, for the launcher: **New app**, **Apps**, **Android apps** with **Get apps**, **Tools** (camera, recorder, translator, the browser and more) and **Settings**. Three bumps come before either end, so a stray tick can't open the launcher or sleep.

A long press reorders agents and pinned apps, or in the launcher, pins an app to home.

## Chats

Hold and talk, or turn the device on its side to type. Replies stream in, tool use shows as a small chip, and some answers bring a card: headlines, weather, a chart. An agent at work in an app or the browser shows it live; touch it to open it full and take over ([Android apps](/docs/android)).

To stop an agent, click twice within 3 s, or say "stop".

## Where your words go

- **Home, a space or a chat:** that agent, in your current chat (in a space, the one under the focus).
- **An app or an Android app:** your last agent, told what's on screen, so "log two" acts on that app.
- **An agent's app or browser, full screen:** the chat using it.

Some need no agent: **System One**, a small model on the device, handles "open water" or "set a timer for ten minutes" as you say them. For "what am I looking at", it opens the camera before you finish, and the photo goes to your agent with your words.

## Approvals

Before an agent changes the device, acts in your accounts, or taps in an app or a page, a card asks: **allow**, **always allow** or **don't allow**. Nothing is focused, so a stray click can't answer; you can also hold and say "allow", "always" or "don't". On the glance screen, a request waits as the first notification.

Granting an app a permission, and an order, payment or message in an Android app, ask every time.

## Typing

Any text box, from a Wi-Fi password to an Android app's search, brings up r1os's keyboard with the device on its side. The wheel moves the cursor; hold the button to type what you say.

## Dock mode

Turn on dock mode (**docked mode** in Settings › Display & sound), and stand the device on its right side, wheel and button down. All of r1os turns landscape, and the glance screen becomes a bedside display: the clock, notifications, widgets, and up to four agents to tap for their chats.

On its side it works by touch (the wheel still turns pages):

- **Unlock:** swipe up from the bottom edge.
- **Home:** swipe up or down between agents and tap one for its chat, or swipe up from the bottom edge for the launcher.
- **Talk:** tap the mic on home, then **send** or **cancel**.
- **Back:** tap a page's header twice.

It stays on while charging, and unplugged on its side above 20% battery. Untouched for two minutes, it **rests**, still and dim, until a touch, the wheel, the button or a notification wakes it. Resting draws about 128 mA, down from 154 mA: 12–13% of the battery an hour. With a PIN set, it locks instead. Adaptive charging, holding the battery near 80%, is planned.
